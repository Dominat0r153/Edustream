# Week 4 Demo Script (60–120 seconds)

1. Start the backend with `npm start` and open `http://localhost:5000`.
2. Click **Launch Dashboard** and sign in with the seeded demo instructor account.
3. Show the dashboard metrics and classroom cards loading from the protected API.
4. Open a classroom and show its title, roster, submissions, and average score.
5. Trigger the Pop Quiz and select an answer.
6. Submit the quiz and show the success/error state, then show the refreshed classroom metrics.
7. Log out and show that protected views require authentication again.
